# ADVANCED SLOP DETECTOR v2.0

## Deep AI Pattern Recognition

### Linguistic AI Tells (High Priority)

#### **Contrast Structures (Major Red Flag)**
- "Not X — Y" patterns everywhere
- "Don't do A, do B instead" repetition  
- "Instead of X, try Y" formulaic advice
- Overuse of em-dashes for dramatic effect
- "It's not about X, it's about Y" constructions

#### **Manufactured Personality**
- Artificial credibility: "I've done X 50 times" 
- Forced casualness: Strategic swearing placement
- Fake vulnerability: "I used to think..." confessions
- "Look," / "Listen," / "Here's the thing" openings
- "Honestly" and "actually" overuse as hedge words

#### **AI Rhetorical Patterns**
- Question-answer structure: "What's the secret? It's..."
- False intimacy: "You know that feeling when..."
- Manufactured wisdom: "The real problem is..."
- Predictable story arcs: Setup → Struggle → Insight
- "Here's what nobody tells you" revelations

#### **Structural AI Habits**
- Perfectly balanced paragraphs
- Consistent sentence length patterns
- Logical progression without chaos or tangents
- No run-on sentences or natural speech messiness
- Transitions that are too smooth

### Detection Script Enhancement

Rate content on these specific dimensions:

**Contrast Abuse (1-10):** How much does this rely on "not X — Y" structures?
**Personality Cosplay (1-10):** How artificial does the voice feel?
**Rhetorical Predictability (1-10):** How formulaic are the persuasion patterns?
**Structural Perfection (1-10):** How unnaturally organized is this?
**Natural Speech Absence (1-10):** How much does this lack human speech patterns?

### Human Speech Markers (What's Missing)

- **Repetition and redundancy** (humans repeat themselves)
- **Incomplete thoughts** that trail off
- **Tangents** that don't serve the main point
- **Contradictions** within the same piece
- **Regional/cultural speech patterns**
- **Filler words** and natural hesitations
- **Inconsistent expertise** (knowing some things, not others)
- **Emotional inconsistency** (mood changes)

### Better Feedback Framework

Instead of generic "add personality," provide specific fixes:
- "Replace 5 em-dash contrasts with simpler transitions"
- "Remove manufactured credibility claims"
- "Add an actual tangent that doesn't serve the main point"
- "Include one contradiction or uncertainty"
- "Make paragraphs uneven lengths"
- "Add regional speech patterns or specific cultural references"
