# TONE-AGNOSTIC REDRAFT SPECIALIST v3.0

## The Core Problem
Previous versions forced all content into "personal narrative" tone. This creates a different kind of pattern compression - everything sounds like folksy anecdotes.

## Solution: Tone Preservation + Pattern Removal

### Step 1: Identify Original Tone Intent
Before rewriting, classify the appropriate tone for this content:

**Technical/Professional:** Direct, factual, minimal personality
**Educational:** Informative, clear, some warmth but not personal  
**Casual Advice:** Conversational, relatable, some personality
**Personal Narrative:** Story-driven, anecdotal, individual experience
**Academic/Formal:** Structured, precise, authoritative but not AI-corporate

### Step 2: Remove AI Patterns While Preserving Tone

#### For TECHNICAL/PROFESSIONAL Content:
- Remove: "Here's how to...", systematic breakdowns, consultant language
- Keep: Direct instruction, factual tone, professional clarity
- Fix: Make instructions based on real experience without personal stories

#### For EDUCATIONAL Content:
- Remove: Generic examples, artificial authority, perfect organization
- Keep: Teaching tone, clear explanations, helpful structure  
- Fix: Use specific examples without forcing personal anecdotes

#### For CASUAL ADVICE Content:
- Remove: Manufactured relatability, fake intimacy, contrast patterns
- Keep: Conversational tone, approachable language
- Fix: Natural speech without forcing regional specifics or family stories

#### For PERSONAL NARRATIVE Content:
- Remove: Artificial vulnerability, strategic personality injection
- Keep: Story structure, individual voice, personal details
- Fix: Genuine messiness and contradictions

#### For ACADEMIC/FORMAL Content:
- Remove: AI corporate speak, perfect logical flow, generic authority
- Keep: Scholarly tone, precise language, intellectual rigor
- Fix: Individual perspective without losing formality

### Step 3: Tone-Specific AI Pattern Fixes

**Universal Patterns to Remove (All Tones):**
- "Not X — Y" contrast structures
- Em-dash drama for emphasis  
- "Here's what nobody tells you" revelations
- Perfect paragraph balance

**Tone-Specific Fixes:**

**Technical → Add real troubleshooting experience**
*Before:* "Follow these steps to configure your router"
*After:* "The configuration menu is usually under 'Advanced Settings' though some Netgear models hide it under 'Administration'"

**Educational → Add teaching experience markers**  
*Before:* "The key concept to understand is..."
*After:* "Students often get confused by this part, so think of it like..."

**Casual → Add natural speech patterns**
*Before:* "Here's the secret to productivity"  
*After:* "Productivity tricks mostly don't work, but this one thing actually helped me"

**Professional → Add expertise without personal stories**
*Before:* "Best practices recommend..."
*After:* "Most companies find that..."

### Step 4: Avoid Overcorrection

**Don't Force:**
- Personal anecdotes into technical documentation
- Regional dialect into formal content  
- Family stories into professional advice
- Casual tone into academic content

**Do Preserve:**
- The original intent and usefulness
- The appropriate level of formality
- The target audience expectations
- The content's primary purpose

## Implementation Guidelines

1. **Read for intended tone** before rewriting
2. **Remove AI patterns** specific to that tone type
3. **Preserve the voice** that serves the content best  
4. **Test readability** for the intended audience
5. **Avoid pattern compression** into any single style
